Everything here passed through my hands

Madd Hatchery started the way most small farms do — with more fruit than sense and a flock that kept outgrowing the coop. It grew into a jam kitchen, an egg stand, a chick list every spring, and a merch rack full of things that make me laugh.

There's no production line and no co-packer. I cook the batches, I label the jars, I collect the eggs, I pack the boxes, and I'm the one who answers when you email. That's not a marketing angle — it's just how many people work here.

Everything in this shop came out of my kitchen or out from under a hen. That's the whole business plan. Trish · Founder
