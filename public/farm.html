A happy flock makes the best eggs

Our birds are spoiled rotten — pasture time, treats, shade, and names they actually answer to. Healthy, happy hens lay the kind of rich, rainbow eggs you can't buy in a store.

That's not sentiment. The flock is the supply chain: no hens, no eggs, no chicks, no hatching eggs. Looking after them properly is the cheapest quality control there is.
