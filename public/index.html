How a jar happens

Picked, cooked, sealed, sent

No factory, no filler, no thickener doing the work that fruit should be doing.

Picked ripe

Fruit goes in at its peak — strawberries, muscadines, blueberries, peppers from the garden out back.

Cooked slow

Small pots, low and slow, stirred by hand until it sets on its own. Nothing added to rush it.

Sealed same day

Hot-filled, sealed and labelled the afternoon it's cooked, so the batch date on the lid is the truth.

Boxed & sent

Wrapped by hand, flat-rate anywhere in the US — or set aside for farm pickup if you're local.
