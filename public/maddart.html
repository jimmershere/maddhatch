Flora & fauna, foraged & finished

Every piece carries where it came from

There's no factory and no two alike. Trish gathers her own materials — off the farm, along the coast, and from a small circle of trusted local hands — then composes each wreath by feel until it's ready to hang on your door.

The speckled bits, the unexpected texture, the tiny vintage trinket tucked into the grapevine — that's the part you can't mass-produce. It's flora & fauna, made into art.

The maker's promise

Built to Trish's standards

The details that make a Madd Art piece an heirloom and not a craft-store wreath.

  • ✦One of a kind. No two pieces are ever the same — each is composed by hand and signed.
  • ✦High-quality adhesives. Built to hold, with materials secured to last.
  • ✦Humanely gathered feathers. Sourced from the flock, mostly at natural moulting.
  • ✦Personalize anything. Colors, names, a theme — requests are genuinely welcomed.
  • ✦Ready to hang. Every piece ships with a complimentary S-hook for easy hanging.
  • ✦Care note. Safe for a covered door — these are natural pieces and not waterproof.
