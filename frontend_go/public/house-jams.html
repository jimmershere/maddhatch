Local fruit, legendary flavor

Every jar carries the story of the growers who nurture our favorite berries. We keep batches small so the muscadine, strawberry, and blueberry notes shine without needing shortcuts.

Muscadine from Cedar Creek Vineyard

These heritage vines in Madison County are hand-trimmed after sunrise and delivered the same afternoon. Their thick skins give our House Muscadine Jam that naturally rich, honeyed finish.

Strawberries from Foxglove Patch

Foxglove’s raised beds stay covered to protect the berries from cold snaps. We pick only the ruby clusters for Sassy Strawberry and Peace Jam, keeping the spice balanced with fresh sweetness.

Blueberries from Riverbend Grove

Riverbend watches soil moisture like hawks so each berry keeps a burst of tartness. The growers text us picking notes so we can time every blueberry batch for peak color and clarity.

From kettle to jar

We taste every pot the minute it cools. Your login unlocks batch logs, including brix readings and spice levels so you can match previous orders.

Flavor board

  • Blueberry Burst: Blueberries brightened with lemon zest for pancakes, yogurts, and parfaits.
  • Peace Jam: A peachy-strawberry blend that sold out at the last three farmer’s markets.
