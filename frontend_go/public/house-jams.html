Local fruit, legendary flavor

Every jar carries the story of the growers who nurture our favorite berries and peppers. We keep batches small so the muscadine, strawberry, blueberry, and pepper notes shine without needing shortcuts.

Hot peppers from Shogun Peppers (GA)

Shogun Peppers hand tends every plant in Georgia, harvesting only once the pods glow red and glossy. Their crew packs and ships the peppers straight to our Madd Hatchery kitchen so the heat tastes garden fresh.

Strawberries from Cottle Farms (Faison, NC)

Cottle Farms crews start before dawn in Faison, North Carolina, to gather dew-cooled berries. Their chill tunnels lock in sweetness so our Sassy Strawberry and Peace Jam stay vibrant without extra sugar.

Blueberries from Moore’s Blueberry Farm (Bridgeton, NC)

Moore’s Blueberry Farm is a family-run u-pick patch near the Neuse River. They rake pine straw around the bushes and share ripeness updates on Facebook, letting us schedule kettles the same morning fruit comes off the rows.

From kettle to jar

We taste every pot the minute it cools. Your login unlocks batch logs, including brix readings and spice levels so you can match previous orders.

Flavor board

  • House Muscadine: A velvety classic that pairs with biscuits or a charcuterie spread.
  • Sassy Strawberry: Strawberries with a little Jalapeño wink for brunch tables that like a spark.
  • Blueberry Burst: Blueberries brightened with lemon zest for pancakes, yogurts, and parfaits.
  • Peace Jam: A peachy-strawberry blend that sold out at the last three farmer’s markets.

Ask for seasonal add-ins — if the growers have a bumper crop, we spin it into limited runs and reserve cases for logged-in members first.
