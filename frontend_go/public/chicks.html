Featured breeds on deck

We rotate a playful lineup of show-stoppers and steady layers. Members get alerts as soon as hatches clear quarantine.

Curly Frizzles

These fluffballs steal every selfie. We keep their curls pristine with high-protein crumble and twice-daily grooming checks so you take home a thriving, social bird.

Ayam Cemani

Midnight plumage, mythic demand. Our Cemani line is tracked for temperament and sheen — log in to see parent pairings and reserve limited hatch spots.

Polish Top Hats

Polish chicks bring the drama with their feathered crowns. We trim around their crests during brooder time so they can find feeders easily once they reach your coop.
