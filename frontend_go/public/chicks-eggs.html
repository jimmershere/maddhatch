Featured breeds on deck

We rotate a playful lineup of show-stoppers and steady layers. Members get alerts as soon as hatches clear quarantine.

Curly Frizzles

These fluffballs steal every selfie. We keep their curls pristine with high-protein crumble and twice-daily grooming checks so you take home a thriving, social bird.

Ayam Cemani

Midnight plumage, mythic demand. Our Cemani line is tracked for temperament and sheen — log in to see parent pairings and reserve limited hatch spots.

Polish Top Hats

Polish chicks bring the drama with their feathered crowns. We trim around their crests during brooder time so they can find feeders easily once they reach your coop.

Table eggs ready for brunch

Pasture time and curated feed keep our eating eggs rich, bright, and sturdy enough for poaching or pickling. We candle every dozen before pickup and track lay dates so your cartons stay consistent.

Silver & Gold Spangled Spitzhauben

Swiss mountain birds with speckled shells that bring a nutty depth to quiches. Their roomy aviary keeps combs pristine and yolks naturally golden.

Easter Eggers

Rainbow baskets, zero dye kits. Our Easter Eggers lay blues, greens, and creams after a forage-heavy diet of seasonal greens and garden herbs.

Silky Curly Frizzles

Yes, the curls lay, too. These gentle birds produce smaller but ultra-rich eggs perfect for baking — we rotate vitamins to keep shells strong.

21 days to hatch, then hands-on care

Healthy chicks take patience. Our incubators run a strict 21-day cycle with logs on temperature, humidity, and egg turning.

Daily care schedule

  • Incubation: Automatic turners keep eggs moving until lockdown, while we chart humidity twice per shift.
  • Hatch window: We candle at day 18, boost humidity, and keep the brooder warmed and ready.
  • First 72 hours: Chicks are checked three times a day for water refreshes, crumble top-offs, and bedding swaps.

Only after those care rounds do we clear birds for pickup or delivery. Logged-in members can see available dates, care notes, and add-on starter kits.
